## What the translator folder does differently

The transcripts here carry a TRIGGER INDEX: every figure, action, question and decision in the source is enumerated and must be matched to a note row or logged as not_mapped — a coverage gate a plain "write the notes" request has no equivalent for. INVENTED items (the pilot year, the roof's "replacing", the "no action" conclusion, the invented deferral, the unstated role titles) have no trigger line to attach to, so the gate would reject them rather than let them land. UPGRADED items keep the source's own hedge — "try", "forecasting", "suspect" — instead of resolving it to a firm verb; a hedge with no matching commitment trigger stays a hedge. ALTERED items cite the transcript's own spelling, wording and figure — "zero" not "Xero", "'29-30" not "2029-30", "about three weeks" not a computed date — rather than a corrected or computed one, since a "corrected" value cannot be pointed at the line that contains it. DROPPED is caught in reverse: every "I'll"/"we'll" and "agreed" is itself a trigger, so an omitted commitment shows as unresolved rather than silently vanishing. Neither note has a MISATTRIBUTED or DROPPED item, but both collect INVENTED, UPGRADED and ALTERED problems that per-line coverage is built to catch.
